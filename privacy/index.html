Privacy Notice

How the firm handles personal data.

Effective September 2, 2026. Written for the brand owners and IP firms the firm serves, many of them outside the United States.

Who is responsible

Crown Hudson Law Firm PLLC, a New York professional service limited liability company, 566 Route 6, Suite 1W, Mahopac, New York 10541, United States, is the controller of the personal data described here. Questions and requests go to nick.harris@crownhudsonlaw.com. The firm is a solo practice and has not appointed a data protection officer; the attorney answers privacy questions personally.

What the firm collects

From clients and the firms that instruct the firm: names, business contact details, the identity of the person authorized to give instructions, the facts needed to prepare and prosecute a trademark matter (ownership, domicile, goods and services, use dates, specimens, prior filings, disputes), correspondence, and payment records. Payment card details are handled by the payment processor and never reach the firm's own systems.

From visitors to this website: the standard server records that GitHub Pages keeps to serve the site, such as the requested page and the requesting network address. The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies and runs no tracking scripts.

From recipients of the firm's outreach: the business email address and firm name published on the recipient's own website, kept with a copy of the message for the period New York's advertising rules require.

Why, and on what basis

To perform the engagement letter with a client, or to take steps at a client's request before one is signed. To meet the firm's legal and professional obligations, including those of the United States Patent and Trademark Office and the New York Rules of Professional Conduct, such as conflict checking under Rule 1.18 and record keeping under Rule 1.15. And for the firm's legitimate interests in running a law practice: keeping accurate files, defending its rights, and telling IP firms that its services exist, which any recipient can stop with one reply.

Where the data goes

The firm works in the United States. Its mail, documents and files are held on Google Workspace, processed by Google LLC under Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum for transfers from the EU, the UK and Switzerland. The firm has accepted those clauses for its account.

Documents filed with the USPTO become part of the public record of the application, available to anyone by serial number; that publication is required by United States law for any registration. The firm shares personal data with no one else except as the client instructs, as the law requires, or with the professionals (an accountant, a successor attorney) the firm engages under a duty of confidentiality.

How long the firm keeps it

A client file is kept for the retention period stated in the engagement letter, then destroyed unless the client asks for it back first. Financial records are kept for seven years as Rule 1.15(d) requires. Intake information is kept indefinitely for future conflict checks, as Rule 1.18 contemplates, whether or not the file is retained. Copies of solicitations and the list of their recipients are kept for three years under New York Rules 7.1 and 7.3.

Your rights

If the EU or UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies to you, you may ask the firm what personal data it holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be erased or its use restricted, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and receive a copy of the data you provided in a portable form. The firm answers within one month. Some requests cannot be granted in full because a lawyer's duties to keep files, check conflicts and preserve the record of a filing override them; the firm will say so and why.

You may complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work. The firm would rather hear from you first and put it right.

The firm has not appointed a representative in the EU or the UK under Article 27. It relies on the exception for processing that is occasional, does not involve special categories of data on a large scale, and is unlikely to result in a risk to the people concerned. If the firm's processing changes in a way that ends that exception, it will appoint one and name them here.

Email

The firm works by email, which is not encrypted end to end. Clients consent to that in the engagement letter and may ask for a different arrangement for particular information. The firm's outreach to IP firms is marked Attorney Advertising in the subject line, carries the firm's name, address and telephone number, and stops on a one-word reply.

Changes

This notice is reviewed when the firm's practices change. The date at the top is the date of the version you are reading.

Questions about your data are welcome.

Write to nick.harris@crownhudsonlaw.com.
