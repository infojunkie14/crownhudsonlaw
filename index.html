Services

What we handle

Federal trademark practice before the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office, for foreign-domiciled brand owners and the firms that represent them.

Foreign applicants

U.S. registration

If your business is based outside the United States, U.S. law requires a U.S.-licensed attorney to represent you before the USPTO (37 C.F.R. § 2.11). We handle the clearance, filing, and prosecution to registration.

Refusals

Office Action responses

Most applications draw a refusal. We respond to § 2(d) likelihood-of-confusion and § 2(e) descriptiveness refusals — and tell you plainly whether a refusal is worth fighting, amending around, or not.

Madrid Protocol

§66(a) provisional refusals

When a Madrid extension of protection to the U.S. draws a provisional refusal, the holder needs U.S. counsel of record. We serve as that counsel on a flat, per-response fee.
