The Practice
01

U.S. registration for
foreign applicants

Businesses domiciled outside the United States must be represented by a U.S.-licensed attorney before the USPTO (37 C.F.R. § 2.11). We handle clearance, class and identification, filing, and prosecution to registration. U.S.-domiciled applicants are welcome as well.

02

USPTO Office
Action responses

Most applications draw a refusal. We answer Office Actions of every kind, § 2(d) likelihood-of-confusion and § 2(e) descriptiveness refusals among them, and advise plainly whether a refusal is worth arguing, amending around, or not.

03

Madrid §66(a)
provisional refusals

When a Madrid extension of protection to the United States draws a provisional refusal, the holder needs U.S. counsel of record. We serve as that counsel on a flat, per-response fee.
