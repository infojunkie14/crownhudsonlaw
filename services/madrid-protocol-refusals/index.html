Madrid Protocol § 66(a) provisional refusals.

Your international registration reached the United States. The USPTO has issued a provisional refusal. This firm answers it.

What happened to your U.S. designation

When an international registration under the Madrid Protocol designates the United States, the USPTO examines that request for extension of protection like any U.S. application. If the examining attorney finds a problem, the USPTO issues a provisional refusal, transmitted to you through WIPO. The refusal arrives with unfamiliar U.S. citations, but it is an ordinary, answerable step in U.S. examination.

Why you need U.S. counsel now

As a holder domiciled outside the United States, you cannot respond through your home-country representative; the response must come from an attorney licensed in the United States (37 C.F.R. § 2.11), appearing as your counsel of record. That is the role this firm takes: we review the refusal, advise on the realistic paths, prepare and file the response, and remain your U.S. counsel of record as the matter proceeds.

How we work with your IP firm

Where an IP firm manages your international portfolio, we work through that firm: it keeps the client relationship and the global strategy, and we carry the U.S. file, reporting in clean, forwardable English at every step. Holders who come to us directly are equally welcome.

What a response can involve

  • Arguments against substantive refusals, including likelihood of confusion under Section 2(d) and descriptiveness under Section 2(e)(1).
  • Amendments to the identification of goods and services, which the USPTO requires to be more specific than the wording the International Bureau accepts, and which in a Section 66(a) application can only be narrowed, never broadened.
  • Disclaimers, translations, and the procedural fixes U.S. examination requires.
  • Plain advice when a narrower designation, or none at all, serves the brand better than a fight.

The fee

Flat, per response, published on the pricing page and confirmed in writing before any work begins. The response period on a Madrid refusal cannot be extended, so send us the refusal the day it arrives.
