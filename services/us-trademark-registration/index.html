U.S. trademark registration for your brand.

From clearance search through examination, handled by U.S.-licensed counsel, on one flat fee quoted in writing.

Why foreign applicants need U.S. counsel

Since August 2019, the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) has required every trademark applicant, registrant, or party to a proceeding domiciled outside the United States and its territories to be represented before it by an attorney licensed in the United States (37 C.F.R. § 2.11(a)). A non-lawyer filing service cannot represent you before the USPTO, and an application filed without qualified counsel draws a requirement to appoint one; left unanswered, the application is abandoned. If your business lives abroad and your brand needs protection in the American market, a U.S. attorney is not optional. This firm exists to be that attorney.

What the engagement covers

  • Clearance search and a candid opinion on risk. Before anything is drafted, we search federal registrations and pending applications and tell you plainly what stands in the way. This is the search that predicts what an examining attorney can cite; a broader search of state registrations and unregistered uses can be quoted on request. If the mark has a serious problem, you hear it before the application is written.
  • Goods and services identification. We draft your identification in USPTO-accepted language and select the international classes it belongs in. Both choices shape what the filing costs and what the registration protects.
  • Filing basis and strategy. A U.S. application can rest on use in commerce, a bona fide intent to use, or a home-country application or registration (Sections 1(a), 1(b), and 44 of the Trademark Act). We recommend the basis that fits how your business operates and say plainly what each requires: an intent-to-use application can register only after you show use in U.S. commerce, while a home-country registration can support U.S. registration without first showing U.S. use.
  • Preparation, filing, and prosecution. We draft and file the application, monitor it through examination and publication, and report each step. If the USPTO issues an Office Action, you get a candid read on it first, and the response is a separate flat fee, confirmed in writing before anything is filed.
  • Plain-language reporting. Every milestone is reported by email, in language a business owner can act on and with the detail a foreign associate needs for the file.

Who this is for

Brand owners domiciled outside the United States, including e-commerce and marketplace sellers whose platforms ask for a U.S. trademark application or registration; international companies entering the American market; and the clients of foreign IP firms that need U.S. counsel of record. The firm also represents applicants domiciled in the United States.

The fee

One flat fee for our work on the application, quoted in a written engagement before any work begins. USPTO filing fees are separate: you pay them to the USPTO directly, from a payment method you store in your own USPTO.gov account, and they never pass through the firm. More than half of attorney-filed applications draw at least one Office Action; if yours does, the response is its own flat fee. There is no hourly billing.
