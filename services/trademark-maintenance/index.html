Trademark lifecycle

Trademark Maintenance and Renewals

Keep an existing U.S. registration on a properly reviewed maintenance schedule.

Existing portfolios are welcome

A firm does not need a new application or a refusal to instruct us. We can assess a scheduled U.S. maintenance filing for an existing registration, including the U.S. declaration required for a Madrid-based registration.

What the engagement covers

The written scope identifies the registration, classes, filing window, required declaration and evidence. We review ownership and actual use for each listed good or service, identify unsupported coverage and prepare the agreed filing for the attorney's review and signature. An optional declaration of incontestability is included only when separately appropriate and agreed.

What we need

The registration number, current owner and instructing-firm details, use information, specimens, any changes to the business, and all notices already received. Do not assume that one specimen proves every item remains in use.

Madrid registrations

U.S. maintenance and renewal of the international registration are different obligations. The written scope identifies which is being handled and who is responsible for the other. We do not describe an international renewal as a U.S. renewal filing.

Deadline and scope review first

Late filings, audits, disputed ownership, excusable nonuse and unusual evidence require individual assessment. Government fees are separate and paid directly to the Office. A completed intake is not acceptance of responsibility for a deadline.
