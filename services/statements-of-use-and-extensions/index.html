Trademark lifecycle

Statements of Use and Extensions

Support for the use-filing stage of an eligible U.S. intent-to-use application.

Choose the right filing from the facts

After a Notice of Allowance, a qualifying intent-to-use application needs a Statement of Use within six months, or an extension; up to five six-month extensions are available, 36 months in all from the Notice of Allowance. We check the application basis, notice, previous extensions and actual use before recommending the appropriate route.

Statement of Use

The agreed service reviews use dates, specimens and the goods or services to be maintained in each class, prepares the filing and identifies any unsupported claims. Future intended use is not a substitute for actual qualifying use.

Extension request

An extension is appropriate only where it remains available and the required intent and other conditions can be truthfully established. We review the full extension history and deadline; the service never assumes another extension exists.

What we need

The application number, Notice of Allowance, all prior use filings and extensions, current goods and services, owner information, use evidence and any approaching date. This route is for eligible Section 1(b) applications, not a way to add an intent-to-use basis to a Madrid application.

Written scope

One filing and its covered classes are identified in the engagement. Division, revived applications, disputed facts and later Office Action responses are assessed separately. Government fees are paid directly to the Office.
