Principal & Counsel of Record

Nicholas K. Harris, Esq.

Every matter at this firm carries one signature, and it is his.

Nicholas K. Harris is a New York attorney, admitted in 2011, and the principal of Crown Hudson Law. The firm practices federal trademark prosecution before the United States Patent and Trademark Office: registrations, Office Action responses, and Madrid Protocol § 66(a) provisional refusals. He also advises on data privacy for clients whose businesses run on data.

The practice is narrow by choice. He appears as counsel of record on every matter and writes to clients in plain language. The firm is a fully online practice: intake, prosecution, and reporting happen in writing, on the client's schedule and in the client's time zone.

The firm is not affiliated with the USPTO or any government agency.

How the firm practices

  • One signature. He is counsel of record on every matter the firm takes, from intake to the last filing.
  • Flat fees, in writing, first. The engagement letter states the fee before the work begins. USPTO filing fees pass through at cost.
  • Candor over volume. A filing with a serious defect, or a refusal not worth fighting, is named as such before anything is filed.
  • Fully online. Instructions, advice, and reports move in writing, so clients abroad and the firms that represent them hold a complete record of every step.
