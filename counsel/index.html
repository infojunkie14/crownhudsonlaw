Principal & Counsel of Record

Nicholas K. Harris, Esq.

Every matter at this firm carries one signature, and it is his.

Nicholas Harris is a New York attorney, admitted in 2011, and the principal of Crown Hudson Law Firm PLLC. The firm practices federal trademark prosecution before the United States Patent and Trademark Office: registrations, Office Action responses, and Madrid Protocol § 66(a) provisional refusals, with related data-privacy counsel for clients whose businesses run on data.

The practice is deliberately narrow and deliberately personal. He appears as counsel of record on every matter, reviews every filing before it is signed, and writes to clients in plain language. The firm is fully online by design: intake, prosecution, and reporting all happen in writing, documented, on the client's schedule and time zone.

The firm is not affiliated with the USPTO or any government agency.

How the firm practices

  • One signature. No matter is delegated past the attorney whose name is on it. What the USPTO receives, he has read.
  • Flat fees, in writing, first. The engagement states the fee before the work begins. Government fees pass through at cost.
  • Candor over volume. A filing with a serious defect, or a refusal not worth fighting, gets named as such before money is spent.
  • Fully online. Email in, email out, everything documented. Built for clients abroad and the firms that represent them.
