For International IP Firms

Trademark support for firms and brand advisers.

For U.S. business law practices, foreign IP firms, and advisers whose clients need U.S. trademark counsel.

For foreign IP firms

Your firm retains its client relationship and leads the global strategy. Crown Hudson represents the holder for the agreed U.S. matter and owes the holder the duties of U.S. counsel. With the holder's authorization, routine instructions and reports pass through your firm, which may also pay our fee. The written engagement records that authority, our scope and a direct-contact route when our duties require it. We never market to a firm's client, and every matter begins with a conflict check.

Defined trademark services

  • New U.S. applications, including registration-risk assessment and goods/services identification.
  • Office Action responses, whether or not we filed the underlying application.
  • Madrid Protocol § 66(a) provisional refusals on U.S. designations.
  • Maintenance, use filings and straightforward ownership updates under their separate service scopes.

Built for cross-border work

The practice is fully online, which suits a firm nine time zones away: instructions and reports move in writing, and each step of the file is documented. Reports explain the U.S. position and the decisions needed from the holder. Turnaround expectations and instruction deadlines are stated in the engagement.

Flat fees, quoted in writing

Fees are flat and quoted in writing per matter type, so your firm knows the U.S. cost before it instructs us. The fee we quote is the fee we charge. USPTO filing fees are separate: they are paid to the USPTO directly from a payment method the holder or your firm stores in its own USPTO.gov account, and they are never invoiced by, paid to or held by this firm.

Read the work before you send a file

A specimen Office Action response sets out, on wholly invented facts, the document this firm drafts and signs at the USPTO, together with the report, the docket entry and the intake questions that accompany it in a real matter.

For U.S. business law firms

If your client needs trademark work outside your practice or an identified matter needs additional capacity, you may introduce the trademark owner for a separate, direct engagement. With the owner's authorization, we can coordinate in writing with existing counsel. The owner remains our client for the agreed trademark matter; your firm's existing relationship is respected.

Applications, Office Action responses and the listed lifecycle filings are the available scope categories. Any arrangement for a law firm to instruct or pay must be expressly agreed in writing. An introduction does not create representation, reserve capacity or automatically qualify for a firm-rate arrangement.

For branding, e-commerce and incorporation advisers

When a client needs U.S. trademark advice, direct the trademark owner to our contact page and, once intake opens, the owner's intake form. The owner identifies you as the referral source. Legal advice, scope decisions and the engagement are handled directly with the owner.

A referral does not authorize an adviser to give trademark advice or instruct us as legal counsel. We offer no referral commissions or percentage of legal fees. This is a prospective referral path, not a claim that any agency, platform or law firm already partners with us.

Begin with a defined need

Intake remains closed. You may write with a general question about a specific service or future collaboration. Please do not send confidential client files or ask us to assume a deadline until conflicts, authority, capacity and a written engagement have been confirmed. No calls or meetings are required.

Write to the firm to open the conversation.

Support for existing U.S. portfolios

Our services include maintenance and renewals, Statements of Use and extensions, and ownership and name updates alongside applications and responses. Each service has a defined written scope. New-matter intake is currently closed.
