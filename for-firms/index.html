For International IP Firms

Your client. Your relationship. Our signature on the U.S. file.

U.S. counsel of record for the firms that manage international portfolios, on flat wholesale fees.

The arrangement

Your clients need a U.S.-licensed attorney for USPTO matters; you need one who treats your client as yours. Crown Hudson Law appears as counsel of record, does the U.S. work, and reports to you. You control the client relationship, the global strategy, and the client communication. We never market to a firm's client, and every matter begins with a conflict check.

What firms send us

  • New U.S. applications for foreign-domiciled clients, including clearance and identification drafting.
  • Office Action responses, whether or not we filed the underlying application.
  • Madrid Protocol § 66(a) provisional refusals on U.S. designations.
  • Counsel-of-record appearances where U.S. representation is required and the file is otherwise yours.

Built for cross-border work

The practice is fully online and email-first, which is exactly what a firm nine time zones away needs: no scheduling calls across midnight, no phone tag, a documented written record of every step. Reports are written in clean English you can forward to your client unedited, and quoted turnaround expectations are stated in the engagement.

Wholesale flat fees

Fees are flat, quoted in writing per matter type, and structured so your firm can bill its client as it sees fit. Fee arrangements comply with the New York Rules of Professional Conduct: we pay no referral commissions and share no fees with non-lawyers, and what we quote is what we charge.

Start with one file

The sensible first step is a single matter: one refusal, one filing. See how the reporting reads and how the deadlines are kept, then decide what to send next.
